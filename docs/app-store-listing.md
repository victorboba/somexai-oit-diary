## Description

OIT Dose Diary is a private diary for oral immunotherapy. It records the dose your allergist prescribed, when it was taken, what else was going on, and any reaction, then turns that into the report your allergist asks for.

LOG A DOSE IN ONE TAP
Today's dose is prefilled from your plan. Mark it taken with food, add cofactors like exercise, illness or poor sleep, or record a skipped day with the reason.

REST PERIOD, HANDLED
Logging a dose starts the after-dose rest countdown and notifies you when it ends. Set a daily reminder for dose time.

REACTIONS, WITH CONTEXT
Symptoms, severity, treatment given, a photo, and how many minutes after the dose it started.

UP-DOSES ON THE RECORD
Record each prescribed dose change after a clinic visit. The report shows every change with its date.

A REPORT YOUR ALLERGIST WILL READ
Adherence over 30, 90 or 180 days, dose changes, reactions with photos, and the full daily log in one PDF. CSV export too.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account. No analytics. Nothing leaves your phone unless you export it.

Works for peanut, milk, egg, tree nut, sesame, wheat and any other allergen, for one child or several.

OIT Dose Diary records what your allergist prescribed. It never changes a dose or tells you whether to take one. Follow your clinic's instructions and contact them with any concern.

## Review notes

OIT Dose Diary is a private, on-device diary for families doing oral immunotherapy under an allergist. It records what the clinician prescribed and produces a PDF report for that clinician. It does not calculate doses or give medical advice. No account is required. After onboarding the app requires the 7-day trial or a purchase; use the sandbox account provided.
